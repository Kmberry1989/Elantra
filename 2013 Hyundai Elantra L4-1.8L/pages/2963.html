Start / Stop Switch: Testing and Inspection





Inspection

1. Remove the crash pad side switch assembly (A) from the switch panel on the crash pad of the driver's side.




2. Remove the ISG switch from the switch assembly.
3. Check the continuity between the switch 2 and 3 terminals as the ISG OFF switch is engaged.
