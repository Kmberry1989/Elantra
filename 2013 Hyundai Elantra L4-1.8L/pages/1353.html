Fogging Sensor: Service and Repair





Inspection

1. Press the MODE switch more then 4 times within 2 seconds while pressing the OFF switch.





* Diagnostic procedure refer to DTC code.

Replacement

1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
2. Disconnect the Sensor cover (A) and then remove the auto defogging sensor (B).




3. Installation is the reverse order of removal.
