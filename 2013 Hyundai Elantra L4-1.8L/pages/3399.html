Turn Signal Lamp





Removal

Door Mirror Turn Signal Lamp
1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
2. Remove the mirror (A) from the mirror holder.





NOTE:
Take care not to damage the mirror cover fixing clip.

3. Loosen the fixing clip and remove the door mirror cover (A).





NOTE:
Take care not to damage the mirror cover fixing clip.

4. Loosen a screw and disconnect a connector (A). Remove the door mirror turn signal lamp (B).






Installation

Door Mirror Turn Signal Lamp
1. Install the door mirror turn signal lamp to the door mirror.
2. Install the door mirror.
3. Connect the battery terminal (-).
