Antitheft Antenna: Service and Repair





Replacement

1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
2. Remove the steering column upper and lower shrouds (A) after loosening the screws (3EA).




3. Disconnect the 6P connector (B) of the coil antenna and then remove the coil antenna (A) after loosening the screw.




4. Installation is the reverse of removal procedure.
