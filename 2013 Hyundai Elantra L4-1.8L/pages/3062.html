Repair Procedures





Replacement

1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
2. Remove the crash pad and heater blower unit.

3. Remove the blower unit (A) from the heater unit after loosening a mounting bolt and screws.





NOTE:
Make sure that there is no air leaking out of the blower and duct joints

4. Installation is the reverse order of removal.
