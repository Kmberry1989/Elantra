Cowl Moulding / Trim: Service and Repair





Replacement

1. Remove the cap (B) and remove the nuts, then remove the wiper arm (A).

Tightening torque :
22.6 - 26.5 N.m(2.3 - 2.7 kgf.m, 16.6 - 19.5 lb-ft)




2. Disconnect the nozzle hose (B).
3. Detach the clips, then remove the cowl top cover (A).




4. Installation is the reverse of removal.

NOTE:
- Replace any damaged clips.
- Slide washer hose back into position after the cowl top cover is installed.
