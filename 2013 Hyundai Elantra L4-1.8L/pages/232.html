Camshaft Bearing Cap





Camshaft bearing cap (A).






Tighten the bolts, in several passes, in the sequence as shown.

Tightening torque
M6 bolts: 11.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.1 lb-ft)
M8 bolts: 18.6 - 22.6 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)






CAUTION: Be careful not to change the position and direction of bearing caps.
