A/C - A/C Compressor Field Coil Specifications

GROUP
HVAC

DATE NOVEMBER 2012
NUMBER 12-HA-003

MODEL(S)
MULTIPLE

SUBJECT:
A/C COMPRESSOR FIELD COIL SPECIFICATION

This bulletin supersedes TSB 07-97-003 to include updated Applicable Vehicles listing.





Description:

For the models in the Applicable Vehicles list below this TSB provides an additional A/C compressor field coil specification that may not be available in the shop manual

Part Specification:

^ Field coil resistance is 3 ~ 4 ohm.





^ Measure between field coil connector and compressor housing ground using DVOM.

^ If the field coil is out of specification, replace it following the applicable shop manual procedure.

Warranty Information:

Normal warranty applies.
