Repair Procedures





Inspection

1. Check the audio remote control switch for resistance between connector 3 and 4 in each switch position.

[Audio (Right)]





[Bluetooth (Left)]






Removal

1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
2. Rotate the steering wheel about 90° clockwise or counterclockwise to remove the screws and airbag module lock pins.








3. Separate the driver side airbag module (A) by pushing three lock pins.








4. Remove to remove the steering wheel remote control switch the screws.








5. Disconnect the steering wheel remote control switch connector(A) after loosening screws.

[Audio (Left)]





[Bluetooth (Right)]






Installation

1. Reassemble the steering wheel remote control switch after connecting the connector.
2. Reassemble the driver airbag module.

NOTE:
Make sure the audio remote control switch and the airbag module connectors are plugged in properly.
