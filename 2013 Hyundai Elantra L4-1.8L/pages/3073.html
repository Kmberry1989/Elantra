Climate Control Air Filter





Replacement

1. Disconnect the guide (B), from the glove box (C) and then remove the glove box left (A).




2. Remove the filter cover (A) with pushing the knob.
3. Replace the air filter, install it after making sure of the direction of air filter.





NOTE:
In case of driving in an air-polluted area or rugged terrain, check and replace the air filter as frequently as possible.

4. Installation is the reverse order of removal.
