Transmission Drain & Refill Locations





Drain the manual transaxle oil after loosening the drain plug (A).






Install the drain plug with a new gasket.

Tightening torque: 58.9 - 78.5 N.m (6.0 - 8.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 57.9 lb-ft)
